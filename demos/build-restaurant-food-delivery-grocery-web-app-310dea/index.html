How it works

From QR scan to door in three taps

The whole flow lives in the browser and WhatsApp — the two apps every phone already has.

    Build the order

FAQ

Questions we get before onboarding

Something else on your mind? Message us — a human replies, usually within the hour.

Do my guests need to install anything?

No. The menu is a web app that opens in any phone browser straight from the QR scan, and WhatsApp is already on their phone. That's the entire stack.

How do orders reach the kitchen?

As a structured WhatsApp message — items, quantities, table label and total. Staff confirm in chat. On Bistro Pro and above, orders can also print in the kitchen or export to your POS.

Can I really use it for grocery delivery?

Yes. The catalog supports units, weights and stock states, plus delivery windows, minimum order values and postcode checks. Codes work on windows, crates and packaging.

What about payments?

Your choice per venue: cash on delivery, a payment link pasted in chat, or integrated card payment links on Pro plans. The order flow never blocks on payment.

Is each QR code unique per table?

Yes — every table, counter or flyer gets its own code. Incoming orders are labeled with where they came from, and you can disable any code instantly (e.g., a closed terrace).
